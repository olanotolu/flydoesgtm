## Design Context

### Users
Founder/creative director exploring and shaping two Clay Entropy product directions. Clay Ghost is the concrete GTM decision tool; Clay Protocol is the future-facing agent trust/reputation concept.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, curious, premium. The landing should feel almost empty and intentional. Ghost should feel tactile, material, and operational. Protocol should feel precise, infrastructural, and calm.

### Aesthetic Direction
Start with a nearly blank warm-white landing page containing two oversized typographic controls: Clay Ghost and Clay Protocol. Use distinct but restrained workspaces: warm clay/oxide tones for Ghost, cool ink/blue tones for Protocol. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI gradients, and decorative density.

### Design Principles
- Let the concept lead; use whitespace and typography before UI chrome.
- Separate the two products visually and semantically, not just by route.
- Make every automation reviewable and explainable.
- Use material cues for Ghost and protocol cues for Protocol.
- Keep real integrations server-side and never expose credentials in the browser.
